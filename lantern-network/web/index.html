SMALL APP. CLEARER PICTURE.

A light on your local network.

Lantern runs on your computer and checks which devices on your local IPv4 network respond to a ping. It uses your computer’s neighbor table for available MAC addresses.

A device that doesn’t respond may be asleep or blocking discovery. “Not seen” doesn’t necessarily mean disconnected. This is a discovery tool, not a complete router client list.

Names and device types can be assigned by you. A trusted label means you recognize a device; it is not a security assessment. Lantern doesn’t read browsing history, measure bandwidth, or block devices.

Supports macOS and Linux. Keep the server running for scheduled scans. The optional background service can keep it running after login. Demo and live records are stored separately in a local SQLite database.
